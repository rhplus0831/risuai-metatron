# Fork feature ledger

This records what became of each feature, policy and idea the fork [surveys](../reports/surveys/README.md)
proposed for Metatron: whether it is in the code, partly in the code, left out by a recorded decision, or still
open. The surveys are snapshots from 2026-10-20 and are not edited; this file is the current state.

It covers the candidate lists of three surveys:

- [HaejeokRisuai](../reports/surveys/haejeok-feature-survey.md): candidates A1–F7, the one small fix of §3 the
  survey does not already mark as fixed, and one suggestion (49 rows).
- [RisuNest](../reports/surveys/risunest-ideas.md): items 1–18, the seven lower-priority items of item 19, and two
  open questions that describe a feature (27 rows).
- [PocketRisu and RisuBard picks](../reports/surveys/pocketrisu-risubard-picks.md): rows 1–19 of §1, the policies
  of §2 and the major features of §3 (53 rows).

## Statuses

- `landed`: the feature, or Metatron's version of the same capability, is in the current source. The evidence
  names the plan in the [archive](../archived-plan/README.md) or the commit that brought it.
- `covered`: Metatron meets the need with a design of its own, so there is nothing to port.
- `partial`: part of it is in the source. The last column says what is not.
- `not planned`: a finished plan's Later, Not In This Plan or out-of-scope list, or an owner's answer, records
  that it was left out. The entry is quoted from [deferred work](deferred-work.md) or the plan.
- `undecided`: [open decisions](open-decisions.md) still lists the question.
- `open`: not in the source, and no decision about it is recorded. This is the list of what could still be done.
- `n/a`: the code it would change is no longer in Metatron.

Evidence paths and identifiers are lookup anchors. Plans are named as the archive names them; read one with
`git show <commit>:<path>` from its archive row.

## Advised against by the surveys

Each survey also lists what it recommends not porting, with the reason. Those lists are the surveys' advice from
2026-10-20, not decisions the owner recorded. They were not checked against the code for this ledger and are not
repeated row by row:

- HaejeokRisuai [§4](../reports/surveys/haejeok-feature-survey.md#4-explicitly-not-worth-porting): the storage
  backends and the sync between them, Tauri and Capacitor, the "pin to generation targets" series, durable model
  jobs, the realtime replay buffer, the inactive-generations asset journal, module sandbox bundles, the
  revoke-on-evict blob cache, the client-side offload helpers, the regex compile cache, the onboarding wizard.
- RisuNest [§2](../reports/surveys/risunest-ideas.md#2-already-covered-or-not-portable): what Metatron already
  covers, and what is tied to Tauri or native code.
- PocketRisu and RisuBard
  [§4](../reports/surveys/pocketrisu-risubard-picks.md#4-what-not-to-bring-over-architectural-conflicts):
  file-native storage, single-writer session locks, the JSON-Patch save protocol, client-side model adapters,
  plain HTTP support, removing group chats, the update-check worker.

## Updating this ledger

- When a plan lands, is retired, or records a Later or Not In This Plan entry that names a survey item, change
  that row here in the plan's close-out commit: the status, the plan or commit, and what is left.
- When the owner decides an `undecided` row, change it here and delete the question from
  [open decisions](open-decisions.md).
- When a new survey adds candidates, add a section with its rows and extend the Baseline counts.
- A row is never deleted. `landed`, `covered`, `not planned` and `n/a` rows are the record that the item was
  handled.
