# Relationship shifts in real-model replays — 2026-11-08

What was measured: whether projecting an event's `relationshipShifts` onto the memory card would carry something the
`relationships` sections do not ([deferred work](../../../backlog/deferred-work.md#structured-memory-section-growth)).
Four exported chats were recorded fresh through the repository's prompt and admission code against a real model, with
the identity replay harness kept outside the repository; the shifts were then grouped by directed pair. Written into the
repository on 2026-11-11 from the session's notes; the replays ran on `dev` after `85991487d`.

- Recording is reliable: 0.7 to 1.1 shifts per event, 73 to 90% of events carry one, and no shift was refused for naming
  a page that does not exist.
- Pairs grow with the cast but all run through the user's character. A five-character office chat gave five directed
  pairs, each with the user's character on one side. The three side characters shifted only toward that character,
  never toward each other, and got pages of their own only in the sixth of the 11 windows.
- The sections stayed small: one or two lines, 54 to 150 characters per page after 11 windows. Each line matched the
  note of the pair's newest shift, so the projection would have produced the same five lines.
- `direction` is free text, from one word to a full sentence, once in another language than the chat.

Reading: the growth that motivated the projection is per page and about the user's character, which the slot budget
already bounds.
